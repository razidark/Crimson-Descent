# CRIMSON DESCENT: COMPLETE DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP
*Gothic Exploration, Gathering, Hunting & Survival Crafting Action-RPG*
**Engine:** Godot 4.7+ | **Style:** 2D Pixel Art ARPG | **Target:** Deep, atmospheric progression

---

## MILESTONE 1: COMBAT MECHANICS & WEAPON ARCHETYPES (IN PROGRESS)
- [x] Base 8-directional movement & shadow dash
- [x] Basic melee cleave attack
- [ ] **Weapon Archetypes (Switch with [Q] or [1/2/3])**:
  - **Blood Blade (Greatsword)**: Slow heavy sweep, high damage, stagger.
  - **Twin Daggers**: Rapid strikes, low stamina, inflicts stacking **Bleed (DoT)**.
  - **Shadow Bow (Ranged)**: Right-click aim, fires arrows crafted from Wood + Sinew.
  - **Soul Catalyst (Magic)**: Launches homing blood orbs / nova explosions.
- [ ] **Combat Polish & "Juice"**:
  - Floating damage numbers (White = normal, Red = Bleed DoT, Gold = Critical Hit).
  - Screen shake trauma on heavy impacts and explosions.
  - Directional hit-knockback and blood splatter particles.
  - Stamina / Energy bar for sprinting and dodging.

## MILESTONE 2: ITEMIZATION, EQUIPMENT & DEEP CRAFTING
- [x] Basic resource gathering (Wood, Ore, Herbs, Meat, Coins)
- [x] Survival crafting menu ([C] / [TAB])
- [ ] **Equipment Paperdoll / Inventory Slots**:
  - Main Hand (Weapon)
  - Off-Hand (Shield / Quiver / Catalyst)
  - Torso Armor (Damage resistance & health)
  - Amulet / Relic (Passive modifiers: +15% Move Speed, Vampiric Leech)
- [ ] **Crafting Stations & Campfire Cooking**:
  - **Campfire**: Cook raw beast meats into restorative meals (Jerky, Stew, Roasted Ribs).
  - **Anvil / Forge**: Smelt raw ore into ingots; forge and upgrade weapons/armor tiers (Copper -> Iron -> Adamantite -> Blood-Infused).
  - **Alchemy Cauldron**: Brew specialized elixirs (Speed Draught, Stoneskin, Anti-Venom).
- [ ] **Tool Progression**:
  - Basic Pickaxe -> Iron Pickaxe -> Blood Pickaxe (mines higher tier minerals).

## MILESTONE 3: MULTI-BIOME WORLD & DUNGEON EXPLORATION
- [ ] **Exploration Layout & Fog of War**:
  - Multi-chamber interconnected map with doors, archways, and corridors.
  - Corner mini-map revealing discovered rooms.
- [ ] **Biome 1: The Crypt Sanctum (Hub)**:
  - Starting safe ruins with your workbench, forge, bed, and campfire.
- [ ] **Biome 2: The Fungus Catacombs**:
  - Overgrown with bioluminescent spores, toxic mushrooms, and lurking spiders.
  - Introduces Poison hazard and Antidote requirement.
- [ ] **Biome 3: The Obsidian Under-Forge**:
  - Scorched stone, lava rivers, fire hounds, and rare adamantite ore deposits.
- [ ] **Secret Vaults & Puzzle Doors**:
  - Locked doors requiring keys hidden in destructible urns or guarded by elites.
